# AI visibility: how it works for a customer

1. **Setup & history.** Enter the brand, website, up to 5 competitors and up
   to 30 questions customers ask AI. Pick engines, country and how often to
   check. Press **Review cost** to see the credits per check and per month,
   then save or run.
2. **A check runs.** Uplumo asks every engine every question and saves each
   answer, the businesses it recommends and the sources it cites.
3. **Overview.** One sentence says how AI treats the brand ("AI names Rival
   3.0× as often as you"), with the weakest engine. Below: answers that name
   you (and the change since the previous comparable check), share of voice
   against competitors, a bar per engine, and the sites AI cites most.
4. **Prompts.** A grid of questions × engines. Each cell says Named, Linked
   only, Not named or No answer. Click it to read what the engine actually
   said, who it recommended and which sources it trusted. Filter to "AI
   leaves you out" to see only the gaps.
5. **Opportunities and Sentiment** explain the gaps and the tone, with an
   optional AI action plan.
6. **Test a question** asks one question live before tracking it.
   **Market research** looks up how often AI already mentions any brand or
   domain. Both show the cost first and run only when you submit.
7. **Prompt ideas** suggests questions to add to the tracker.
