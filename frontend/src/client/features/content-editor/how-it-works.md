# How the Content Editor works

1. Open an existing project draft or start a new one. The title, target topics, URL slug, search description, and article body are edited separately.
2. The score panel checks the current writing. SEO and AI-answer modes use the article and any matching brief; scores describe writing checks, not expected Google rankings.
3. Get a search brief when useful. On an eligible paid plan, a matching cached brief uses no extra credits. Building or refreshing a brief asks the search provider to read ranking pages and costs up to the amount shown before the action.
4. AI help returns a suggestion. Nothing enters the article until you choose Insert or Replace. Review generated facts before publishing.
5. Preview the reader view at desktop or mobile width. The title appears once even when the body begins with the same H1.
6. Not on WordPress? In the Publish tab, copy the article as HTML for your site's editor, or download it as an .html page (upload it with your host's file manager) or a Markdown file.
7. Paste or upload your blog's CSS under **My blog theme**. The preview can then show the article in your own design, and the .html download can carry that design.
8. Edits autosave after a short pause. You can also use **Save draft**. WordPress publishing uses the last saved version and requires an existing site connection.

Use the brief's keyword research to compare volume, difficulty and intent in your project market. Add relevant suggestions to your target topics; do not add unrelated high-volume terms. **Analyze full draft** reviews intent, missing answers and evidence without replacing the article. Revise the highest-priority findings, then review again only when needed. If published content does not gain visibility, check indexing and query/page performance in the existing search tools before buying another brief; the writing score is not a ranking measurement.

## Edit a page that is already live

Next to a page in Site Audit, in an AI answer that cites your site, or in Search Growth, press **Edit in Content Editor**. Uplumo copies that page's title, headings and text into a draft and opens it, so you can fix it right away. Press it again later and the same draft opens with your edits. Only pages of the project's own website can be opened this way, and it costs no credits.
