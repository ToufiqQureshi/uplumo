# How the Content Editor works

1. Open an existing project draft or start a new one. The title, target topics, URL slug, search description, and article body are edited separately.
2. The score panel checks the current writing. SEO and AI-answer modes use the article and any matching brief; scores describe writing checks, not expected Google rankings.
3. Get a search brief when useful. On an eligible paid plan, a matching cached brief uses no extra credits. Building or refreshing a brief asks the search provider to read ranking pages and costs up to the amount shown before the action.
4. AI help returns a suggestion. Nothing enters the article until you choose Insert or Replace. Review generated facts before publishing.
5. Preview the reader view at desktop or mobile width. The title appears once even when the body begins with the same H1.
6. Edits autosave after a short pause. You can also use **Save draft**. WordPress publishing uses the last saved version and requires an existing site connection.
