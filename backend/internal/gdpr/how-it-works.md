# Account deletion for users

Open **Settings → Personal → Delete my account**. During setup, the same action is in the account menu. You can choose an optional reason and confirm once. If your sign-in is older than 15 minutes, sign in again and confirm.

Deletion removes your account, sessions, and workspaces you own alone. Payment records are retained for accounting. A shared workspace requires leaving or transferring ownership first. A running subscription or an open payment must be resolved before deletion.
