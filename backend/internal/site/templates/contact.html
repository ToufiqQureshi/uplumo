Choosing Uplumo for your business

Tell us what kind of website you run, whether you work on client sites, and which task you want to improve: AI visibility, technical fixes, research, content or reporting. We can explain the relevant workflow and plan limits before you decide to pay.

To get a faster answer

  • Write from the email address you signed up with.
  • For a billing question, include the date and amount of the payment.
  • For a problem on a page, tell us which page and what you expected to see. Include the approximate time and a screenshot with private information hidden.
  • Never send passwords, API keys, card details, Google access tokens or one-time codes. A payment reference is enough to help us locate a billing issue.

Common requests

  • Cancel a paid plan. You can do this yourself in one click from Billing. See the cancellation and refund policy.
  • Delete your account and data. Email us from your account's address and we erase it. See the privacy policy.
  • Our crawler visited your site. Site audits use the "Uplumo-Audit" user agent and obey robots.txt. Write to us if you want it to stop.
