02 / AI Visibility

Are they finding you
or your competitors?

Use Brand Lookup to investigate mentions and cited pages in supported AI datasets. Add competitors to compare your share of voice, then inspect the questions and sources behind the numbers.

  • Review brand mentions and the pages being cited.
  • Compare competitors in the selected market.
  • Save questions in the tracker to measure them over time.

What you get: evidence of where your brand appears and which competing sources deserve a closer look.

Brand Lookup uses supported ChatGPT and Google AI Overview datasets. Engine and market coverage differ; it does not monitor private conversations.

03 / Prompt Explorer

One customer question.
Different AI answers.

Ask a selected question across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. Read the answers and source links in one place, and highlight a brand to see whether it appears.

  • Choose the models, question and supported search market.
  • Inspect the answer text and citations for each result.
  • Separate a brand mention from a link that actually cites it.

What you get: a concrete answer to “what does this AI response say about us?”

API-generated answers can differ from consumer chat apps. This is a check of your selected prompt, not every possible conversation. Fresh checks use credits.

04 / Cite Fix

A score is a start.
The fixes are the point.

Turn a completed website audit into a prioritised citation-readiness checklist. See which pages need attention and why, then request focused AI help for the pages you choose.

  • Check readable content, identity, freshness, answer structure and sources.
  • Inspect affected URLs and available saved citation evidence.
  • Use optional Quick analysis or Deep rewriting, then open the draft in the editor for review.

What you get: specific page improvements you can review and act on instead of another unexplained score.

The checklist is diagnostic, not an official AI score or a guarantee of citations. Reading saved checks is free; optional AI and fresh provider checks show their credit limits first.
