A practical workflow

From “why am I not visible?” to a page you can improve.

Work with your own website and evidence. Choose paid checks when you need them; review the credit information before starting.

01 · Investigate

Ask the questions your customers ask.

Use AI Visibility to inspect sampled answers and their sources. Track selected questions by engine and market, and keep brand mentions separate from actual citations.

02 · Diagnose

Use Cite Fix to prioritise page improvements.

Start with a completed site audit. Inspect readability, identity, freshness, answer structure and source signals, alongside saved citation evidence and available rival-page comparisons. The readiness score explains Uplumo checks; it does not predict whether an AI will cite you.

03 · Improve

Keep your judgement in the writing process.

Edit your own content, add images and use search briefs or focused AI suggestions. Optional Cite Fix analysis works on a selected page. Review facts and proposed changes before applying or publishing to a connected WordPress site.

04 · Measure

Check what changed, then report the work.

Follow rankings, saved AI answers and website traffic. Connect your Google data where available. Record actions and observed outcomes in a client report; a change after an edit is evidence to investigate, not proof of causation.

Feature access, engine coverage and usage depend on your plan and configuration. Reading a saved Cite Fix report makes no new paid AI or search-data request. Optional AI analysis, fresh AI Overview checks and JavaScript rendering are separate credit-using actions.
