AI visibility tracker

Measure your brand’s AI search visibility in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews. Compare individual answers and follow important questions over time.

An AI visibility tracker asks AI assistants the questions your customers ask, saves each answer and records whether it mentions your brand and which pages it cites. Uplumo runs your saved questions on ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Claude and Perplexity on a daily, weekly or monthly schedule.

Choose questions buyers actually ask

Start with product comparisons, category recommendations and questions about your brand. Save the prompts that matter to your business and keep the engine and market consistent when comparing results.

See the difference between a mention and a citation

Read the captured answer and inspect its sources. A mention shows that the answer names your brand. A citation shows that it links to a page. Track both to see whether you are getting recognition, links, or both.

Find where competitors appear

Compare mentions across your selected prompts and inspect the sources behind competitor recommendations. If competitors show up on a comparison question and your site is absent, use the cited pages to identify which buying questions your content could answer.

Plans and availability

Results describe sampled answers. Engine, market and feature availability can vary. Compare like-for-like prompts and read the answer text before acting. Compare plans and limits.
