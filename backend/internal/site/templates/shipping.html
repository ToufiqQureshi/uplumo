Nothing is shipped

Uplumo is online software. We do not sell or send physical goods, so there are no shipping charges, couriers or delivery addresses.

How you get the service

  • Free plan. Your account works as soon as you sign up and confirm your email address.
  • Paid plans. Your plan is switched on for your workspace as soon as your payment succeeds, usually within a few minutes.
  • You use Uplumo in your browser at uplumo.com. There is nothing to download or install.

If your plan is not switched on

If you have paid and your workspace still shows the Free plan after 30 minutes, email {{.Support}} from your account's address with the date and amount. We switch it on or refund the payment, as set out in the cancellation and refund policy.

What account access includes

Your workspace is the place to add a website, connect supported accounts and use the tools included in your plan. Google data needs your authorisation, analytics needs the tracking script installed, and WordPress publishing needs a connection you control. Account activation does not automatically configure these services for you.

Audits, scheduled checks and provider-backed research can take time after you start them. A paid subscription provides the listed tools and allowances; it is not delivery of a guaranteed ranking, AI citation, traffic increase or sale.
