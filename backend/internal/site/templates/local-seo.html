Local SEO tools for Google Maps

Compare your Google Business Profile against leading Maps results for a local query. See which listings customers encounter and which profile details you can improve.

Local SEO tools show how your business appears in Google Maps and local search compared with nearby competitors. Uplumo checks Maps for a search at your address and compares your Google Business Profile with the top three businesses there.

Compare your profile with local competitors

Start with a service and market, such as emergency dental care in your city. Run a profile check and compare your listing with the top three Maps results returned by the check. Look at public ratings, review counts, and profile details to decide what to investigate first.

Review feedback before updating your listing

Use the review analysis to inspect recurring themes and unanswered reviews. Look for actionable gaps, such as customers asking about appointment hours that your profile could clarify. Check any proposed reply against the original review before posting.

Track changes in local search

After a profile check, you can enable weekly monitoring for the identified competitors and see the credit allowance before you start. Maps results can vary with the searcher's location and the query used, so compare consistent checks over time.

Plans and availability

Local Visibility is available on Pro and Business. Profile checks and optional monitoring use workspace credits. Compare plans and limits.
