Google keyword rank tracker

Monitor Google rankings for the search terms that bring potential customers to your website. See position changes over time and use keyword research to choose what to track next.

A keyword rank tracker checks where your pages appear in Google for the searches you choose, on a schedule, and keeps the history so you can see positions move. Uplumo checks weekly on every paid plan, with optional daily checks.

Pick keywords that match business goals

Start with the queries that describe your products and services. Mix branded terms with discovery queries so you can see how often people find you before they know your brand.

Review ranking changes over time

Monitor your tracked keywords on the plan’s check schedule. When a priority keyword drops, compare its recent history with changes to your landing page and the pages currently appearing in search.

Use rankings alongside first-party search data

Connect Google Search Console on supported plans to review clicks and impressions. Compare those numbers with rank changes before deciding which pages to improve.
