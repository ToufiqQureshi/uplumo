What the audits check

Two engines, one page check.

The SEO and AEO audits read the same page once and score it separately. Every check shows what we found, why it matters and the fix. Nothing is guessed: if we cannot read something, we say so.

SEO audit

Can Google crawl, index and rank it?

HTTPS, noindex, Googlebot access, redirects, canonical and hreflang tags, the XML sitemap, titles, headings, links, speed for real visitors, structured data and content length.

AEO audit

Can ChatGPT read, trust and quote it?

AI crawler access, text without JavaScript, questions as headings, direct answers, lists and tables, figures, sources, quotes, author and date, about and contact pages, and business schema.
