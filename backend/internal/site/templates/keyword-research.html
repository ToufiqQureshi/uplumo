SEO keyword research tool

Find Google searches worth targeting and see what ranks before you write a page. Uplumo combines keyword ideas, market-level estimates, and search result analysis.

Compare keyword ideas in your target market

Start with a topic and choose the market you sell to. Review related terms, estimated monthly search volume, and reported difficulty. Volume estimates describe search demand. Use them alongside your knowledge of which searches lead to a sale.

Check what Google ranks for each query

Look at the current search results before choosing what to publish. For example, if a software query shows mostly comparison guides and a related query shows product demos, give each searcher the page format they expect. When two terms return similar results and ask the same question, use one useful page for both.

Use search demand to choose what to track

Choose a primary query, a URL, and the market you want to monitor. Use the Google keyword rank tracker to follow position changes and check Search Console for the queries that actually bring impressions and clicks.
