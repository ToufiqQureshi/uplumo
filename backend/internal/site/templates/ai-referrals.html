AI referral tracking

See when AI assistants send people to your website and which pages they land on. Uplumo Analytics is available on the free plan.

AI referral tracking counts the visits that reach your website from AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, and shows which pages they land on. It sees a visit only when the AI app passes a referrer or the link carries a campaign tag.

Add the tracker to your website

Create a project and add the Uplumo tracking script to the pages you want to measure. The tracker is cookie-free and recognises supported referrers and campaign tags.

Find pages receiving AI traffic

Review visits by source and landing page. If your comparison guide is attracting identifiable AI visits, check whether the guide answers the next buying question and links to a relevant product or signup page.

Compare traffic with visibility

Use AI Visibility to see where your brand or pages appear in sampled answers. Use Analytics to see which identifiable visits reach your site. Reviewing both helps you compare exposure with observed visits.

Plans and availability

When an AI app or browser strips the referrer and there is no campaign tag, the visit remains Direct or unknown. Compare plans and limits.
