About Uplumo

Understand your visibility. Know what to improve.

SEO, AI search visibility and website analytics in one workspace for businesses, independent marketers and agencies.

Built from search experience and hands-on engineering

Uplumo brings together 3 years of experience in SEO and answer engine optimisation (AEO), backed by full-stack AI engineering experience. That combination shapes how we build: start with the question a business needs answered, inspect the available evidence, and turn it into a practical next step.

SEO helps people discover your website through search engines. AEO focuses on making information easier for answer engines to understand and reference. Uplumo helps you work on both, while keeping observed results separate from suggested improvements.

Why Uplumo exists

A ranking chart alone does not tell you which page needs attention. An AI mention alone does not tell you whether anyone visited your website. A long audit can leave you with more work and no clear starting point.

We built Uplumo to connect research, measurement and the work of improving a website. You can investigate search demand, inspect sampled AI answers, review technical issues, improve a draft and report the work from the same project. Plans are priced in Indian rupees, with tool access and credit allowances explained before you subscribe.

From evidence to action

  • AI Visibility: investigate brand mentions, cited pages and competing sources. Compare selected prompts across supported AI models and save questions for repeat measurement. Coverage depends on the engine, country, language and available provider data.
  • Cite Fix: use a completed site audit to inspect readability, business identity, author and freshness signals, direct answers, supporting sources and content depth. Prioritised checks and saved citation evidence help you decide which pages to improve. Optional AI page analysis and rewriting use credits.
  • Search and traffic: connect read-only Google Search Console and Google Analytics reports, or install Uplumo Analytics to measure page visits, sources and configured events. Recognised AI referrals are reported separately from AI mentions.
  • Research and tracking: explore keywords, search results, domains and backlinks, then follow selected Google rankings over time. Research estimates and location-specific results need context; they are not promises of future performance.
  • Content workflow: prepare search briefs, edit your own drafts, upload images and request focused AI help. Review suggestions before applying them, and publish through a connected WordPress site when ready.
  • Client reporting: record completed work, observed outcomes, evidence and next steps. Create a share link when you want a client to review a report; revoke sharing when it is no longer needed. White-label branding is available on the eligible plan.

Who it is for

Business owners who want to understand their website before choosing the next improvement. SEO specialists and content writers who need evidence for research and editing. Agencies who manage several projects and need to explain their work to clients.

What we measure, and what we do not promise

An AI brand mention, a link cited in an answer and a visit to your site are different events. We do not combine them into a claim that a citation caused a sale. AI visibility checks are samples of selected questions or provider datasets, not a record of every private conversation.

Content and citation-readiness scores are Uplumo's diagnostic rules, not scores issued by Google or an AI assistant. No tool can guarantee a first-page ranking, inclusion in an AI answer or new revenue. Our job is to help you inspect evidence and make better-informed improvements.
