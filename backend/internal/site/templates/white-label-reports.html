White label SEO reports

Send clients SEO reports under your agency’s name, logo and colour, built from their own Search Console, Analytics, ranking, audit and backlink data.

A white label SEO report is a client report that carries your agency’s branding instead of the software vendor’s. In Uplumo, you set your brand once for the workspace and every shared report uses it.

Build a report from the client’s data

Pick the sections you need: Google Search performance, organic traffic from Google Analytics, keyword rankings, site health and backlinks. Choose the last 7, 28 or 90 days. Each section reads the data source connected to that client’s project.

Explain the work, not only the numbers

Add a work log with the work completed, the outcomes you observed and the next steps. Clients see what changed and what you plan to do, next to the evidence.

Share a branded link

Share the report as a link. The page shows your brand name, logo and accent colour, says it was prepared by your agency and can link to your website. Turn the link off at any time.
