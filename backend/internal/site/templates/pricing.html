Start with Free

Try website analytics, the starter audit and the content editor. It is a way to explore the product, not a trial that automatically becomes a paid subscription.

Use Basic for one website

Choose research, Google data, rank tracking and writing tools for a single site. Pro adds AI Visibility, backlink and domain research, and room for several projects. Business adds white-label reporting and higher project and team limits.

Plan your credit use

Credits are shared by your workspace. For example, running rankings and then requesting an AI answer uses the same allowance, rather than a separate balance for each tool. Scheduled work also uses that balance. Check the in-app cost information and Billing forecast for your actual inputs.

A keyword capacity is how many keywords you can save; it is not a promise that every possible device, market and scheduled run is covered by your monthly credits. No paid action continues once the available credits cannot cover it. Changing plan currently requires contacting support.
