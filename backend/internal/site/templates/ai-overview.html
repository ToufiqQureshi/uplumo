Google AI Overview tracker

See whether Google’s AI Overviews name your brand or cite your pages for the searches your customers make, in the market you sell to.

A Google AI Overview tracker runs the searches you choose on Google, saves the AI Overview when Google shows one, and records the brands it names and the pages it cites. Uplumo runs these checks on a schedule, so you can see which searches mention you and which name competitors instead.

Choose the searches that lead to a sale

Start with the questions buyers type before they choose a provider: comparisons, “best” lists and problems your product solves. Pick the country and language you sell in, because Google can show a different overview, or none, in each market.

Read the overview and its sources

Open a saved check to read the overview text and the pages Google cited. If a competitor’s guide is cited for a question your page answers, compare the two pages: what the cited page states plainly, which facts it gives and how recently it was updated.

Compare Google with other AI assistants

The same saved questions can run on ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity. Comparing engines shows whether a gap is specific to Google or shared across assistants.

Plans and availability

Google AI Overview tracking is part of AI Visibility. Choose daily, weekly or monthly checks and see the credits each check uses before you start. Compare plans and limits.
