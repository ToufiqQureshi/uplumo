# Public careers rules

The owner requested exactly three openings. Role descriptions are editorial content in the careers template, not inferred from visitor data. Applications reuse the site's existing support address; no unverified recruiting mailbox is invented.

Salary, location, employment type, hiring deadlines and benefits are unspecified. Discuss these with applicants; do not invent commitments or JobPosting structured data that requires unconfirmed details. No paid API, database migration, application upload or automatic email is introduced.
