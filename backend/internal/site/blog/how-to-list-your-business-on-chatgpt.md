## Is ChatGPT Business Hub better than Google Business Profile?

ChatGPT has an advantage when customers are still deciding what they want. A guest might ask, “Which family resorts have indoor activities for young children on rainy days?” Then they can ask which options offer an airport shuttle or how to check the cancellation policy. That back-and-forth gives a business more ways to be discovered while a customer compares options. OpenAI reported 1.2 billion weekly ChatGPT users at launch. That number describes overall platform usage, and OpenAI did not provide a forecast for how many users will see an individual listing. [OpenAI launch announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)

| What the customer needs | ChatGPT Business Hub | Google Business Profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explore and compare options | ChatGPT answers detailed questions and supports follow-ups. Eligible businesses can offer a dedicated Business Agent. | Customers can compare businesses directly in Google Search and Maps using profile details, photos, and reviews. |
| Act on a nearby choice | Customers can open a business profile when it appears in a response and follow a website link. | Customers can get directions, call the business, or visit its site directly from the profile. |
| Measure discovery | Aggregate mentions, website clicks, and discovery topics are available subject to reporting thresholds. | Performance reporting can include search terms, profile views, directions, calls, and website clicks, where applicable. |

For hotels and other local businesses, keep Google Business Profile current and claim the ChatGPT profile if eligible. For an online SaaS like Uplumo, conversational comparisons are a clear reason to test ChatGPT visibility. A buyer can describe their budget, team size, and reporting needs in the same question. ## How to claim and verify your business on ChatGPT

1.  **Find the business.** Sign in to [Business Hub](https://chatgpt.com/business-hub) and search by name. Select the existing profile if it matches. If no listing exists, look for an option to add one. Claim a business only if you own it or are authorized to represent it.
2.  **Use a verification method offered for your profile.** OpenAI may offer a business phone, email, website domain, or Shopify verification. For domain verification, add the TXT record provided by ChatGPT to the domain’s DNS, then return to verify it. DNS updates can take time to appear.
3.  **Complete the approval and identity checks.** OpenAI reviews submissions. Persona identity verification is required to publish changes, view insights, connect a feed, or create an eligible agent. OpenAI says it may ask for identification, registration details, or proof of authority. The blue check on the public profile appears after Persona verification is approved.
4.  **Update the profile.** Once approved, check the website link, business description, category, logo, and relevant policies or photos. OpenAI reviews profile updates, and changes can take time to appear.

### Where is ChatGPT Business Hub available?

Eligible businesses can claim a profile where ChatGPT and the required verification are available. Location and business type both matter. OpenAI says eligible online businesses may see an option to add a profile. Its setup guide says local stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing U.S. listing. For a business with physical locations, check each location’s eligibility in Business Hub before planning a rollout. [OpenAI eligibility guidance](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)

## What ChatGPT business insights tell you about AI mentions

Once the required approvals are complete, Insights shows aggregate activity over the past 28 days. There’s an important detail for online businesses. ChatGPT can name your brand in an answer without recording a mention in Business Hub.

| Metric | What it measures | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mentions | For online businesses, ChatGPT must reference the website. For local businesses, a result showing that location can count even without a click. | Track your own reported visibility over time. Read the count using the definition for your business type. |
| Website clicks | Clicks from ChatGPT through to your website. | Use your site analytics to see what visitors did after arriving, such as signing up or starting a booking. |
| Discovery topics | Common themes in answers where ChatGPT mentioned or cited the business. | Use themes to spot questions worth answering more clearly on your own website. |

Reporting thresholds and data availability can leave metrics blank, and counts may be incomplete. OpenAI withholds individual prompts and consumer conversations, including chats with Business Agents. [OpenAI’s definition of profile insights](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)

### Can it show who ChatGPT recommends instead of you?

OpenAI’s published Business Hub documentation describes your own mentions, clicks, and discovery topics. It does not document a report that tells you which competitor appeared when you were absent, which sources supported that recommendation, or your share of recommendations across a category. Discovery topics cover answers in which your business already appeared. If your hotel never appears for weekend-break questions, that gap may be invisible in this report.

The documentation also does not describe a prioritized plan for earning more mentions. A theme like “family stays” can tell you where you already show up. It gives you no diagnosis of why a competing property gets cited for “family resorts with indoor activities.” To investigate that, test the specific question and inspect the sources returned. [OpenAI profile insights documentation](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)

## What else does ChatGPT offer businesses?

### Business profiles, retail product feeds, and team access

Claiming a profile gives eligible businesses a way to maintain the information they submit. Retailers can connect supported product feeds, including Shopify or a compliant CSV, to share product details such as price and availability. A Business Hub admin can also invite teammates and, where supported, grant an agency access. Distinct brands are managed separately, and claiming a parent brand does not claim all of its locations. [OpenAI’s Business Hub setup guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)

### Business Agents

A Business Agent can answer questions using information a company supplies, such as product specifications, return policies, or service details. Users choose whether to open a clearly labeled conversation with it. OpenAI initially offers agents to eligible U.S. ecommerce and online service businesses with a website. The current agent can explain an offering and send customers to the business website. It cannot complete purchases, make bookings, or check order status in the current experience. [OpenAI’s guide to Business Agent capabilities](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)

## What to do after claiming your ChatGPT business listing

1.  **Build a baseline with realistic buyer questions.** Include unbranded prompts such as “Which family resorts near the airport have indoor activities for kids?” and comparison prompts such as “Which tools track ChatGPT citations and connect GA4 for a small marketing team?” Save the date, question, location or market, and whether web search was used. Use similar conditions and repeat tests because answers can vary.
2.  **Record the brands and sources in the answers.** Track whether your business was named, whether your site was cited, which alternatives appeared, and what sources were linked. If you report share of visibility, give the denominator and label it as a sample. Appearing in 6 of 20 tested answers is 30% of that sample.
3.  **Fix one gap you can verify.** If competing hotel answers cite pages that explain indoor activities and your own amenities page has no detail, add accurate descriptions, operating hours, and relevant photos. If SaaS answers cite competitor pricing pages, check whether your own plans, currency, and limits are clear and current. Confirm your relevant pages are accessible to OAI-SearchBot if you want them eligible for ChatGPT Search. [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)
4.  **Measure what happened after the click.** In GA4, use Traffic acquisition to look for identifiable ChatGPT referral traffic. Compare landing pages and key events such as a signup, enquiry, or booking start. Business Hub clicks and GA4 sessions measure different events and can differ.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to buy ads or publish an agent to claim my business?

No. You can claim an eligible business and complete verification independently of Ads Manager and Business Agents. [OpenAI setup FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)

### Does claiming help my business rank higher in ChatGPT?

Claiming gives you a way to submit and update accurate information. ChatGPT makes its own recommendations, and OpenAI does not guarantee increased visibility or sales. [OpenAI Business Hub FAQ](https://openai.com/business/businesses-in-chatgpt/)

### Does ChatGPT show my business only after I claim it?

ChatGPT can already show information about an unclaimed business when relevant. Claiming gives an authorized representative access to manage submitted information and, after the required approvals, see available insights. [OpenAI Business Hub FAQ](https://openai.com/business/businesses-in-chatgpt/)

### Does a blank mentions report mean my brand never appeared?

A blank metric may reflect a reporting threshold, collection still in progress, or data unavailability. An online brand name can also appear without a website reference, which OpenAI excludes from its online-business mention count. [OpenAI insights FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)
