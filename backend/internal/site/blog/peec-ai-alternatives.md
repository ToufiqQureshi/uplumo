## Price per tracked prompt

Monthly prices are hard to compare when prompt limits differ. Dividing price by prompts tracked gives a fairer first look.

![Bar chart of monthly price per tracked prompt: Peec AI $1.90, Otterly $1.93, Scrunch $2.00, SE Ranking $2.67, Goodie $2.68, Semrush $3.98](/site/blog/aeo-tools-2027-price-per-prompt.webp)

*Entry plans, billed monthly. Engines, refresh rate and extras differ by tool.*

On unit price, Peec AI is already competitive at about $1.90 a prompt. The better reason to switch is a feature Peec does not cover for you: more engines on each prompt, site audits, content recommendations, or a link from citations to leads.

## Five checks before you switch

![Five checks for any Peec AI alternative: engines, prompts, evidence, markets and leads](/site/blog/peec-ai-alternatives-checklist.webp)

*Ask every vendor the same five questions.*

1. **Engines.** Which models are tracked on each prompt, and can you choose them?
2. **Prompts.** How many are included, and what does the next 100 cost? Otterly charges $99 for 100 extra prompts.
3. **Evidence.** Can you open the raw answer and the exact cited URLs, or only a score?
4. **Markets.** Can you set country and language per prompt? Peec AI lists multi-country on its Advanced plan.
5. **Outcomes.** Can the tool connect a citation to a visit or lead on your site?

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Peec AI cost?

Peec AI lists Starter at $95 a month for 50 prompts, Pro at $245 for 150 prompts and Advanced at $495 for 350 prompts, each with three models and unlimited users.

### What is the cheapest Peec AI alternative?

Otterly.AI Lite costs $29 a month for 15 prompts on four engines. Its price per prompt is close to Peec AI's at about $1.93.

### Can I move my Peec AI prompts to another tool?

Yes. Copy the prompt text, engines and markets exactly, and run both tools on the same panel for two weeks so your trend line stays comparable.
