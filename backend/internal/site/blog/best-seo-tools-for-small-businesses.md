## Build a toolkit around one problem

### Your site gets few impressions

Check Search Console for indexing issues and the queries you already appear for. Use a crawl to spot links that lead to redirects, pages with generic titles, and priority URLs that are hard to reach. The [website SEO audit tool](/seo-audit-tool) groups issues by affected URL so you can work through the important pages first.

![Website visibility diagnosis diagram that separates indexing, impressions, clicks, and leads](/site/blog/google-ranking-diagnosis.webp)

*Start at the first stage where your website loses visibility.*

### Competitors rank for keywords you want

Inspect the search results for a short list of buying queries. A keyword such as “emergency dentist” may bring up a local map and service pages. An informational query such as “what to do with a cracked tooth” can need a different page. Use the page formats in the results to decide which queries belong together. Then use a [Google keyword rank tracker](/keyword-rank-tracker) to monitor your chosen terms in the same market over time.

### Leads come from Maps or AI answers

If nearby customers drive the business, prioritize local visibility across the areas you serve and check whether listing visits turn into calls or bookings. For AI discovery, measure two signals separately: sampled answers that cite your website, and visits you can identify as AI referrals. ## Test the tools on one page

1. **Pick a page with a business goal.** Choose a service, product, or comparison page. Define one conversion such as a call, signup, or booking request.
2. **Capture a baseline.** Record the page's impressions and clicks from Search Console. Note any tracked keyword positions and the period for your conversion totals.
3. **Ask each tool for the same evidence.** Check whether it can show the page's technical issue, ranking query, competitor page, or source of a visit. Save the affected URL and the date of the check.
4. **Make one change you can explain.** A service page with impressions for pricing questions may need clear fees or pricing ranges. A page that Google cannot access needs a crawl or indexing fix first.
5. **Compare the same measurements afterward.** Look for changes in impressions, clicks, and conversions. Note seasonality, technical releases, and changes in competitors' pages before attributing the result to your edit.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best free SEO tool for a small business?

Start with Google Search Console for your own indexing and Google search performance. Add free analytics for conversions and a starter crawler to inspect technical issues.

### Do small businesses need to pay for SEO software?

Pay when a recurring job such as competitor research, tracking a stable list of keywords, or reporting across multiple sites takes more effort than your free tools can handle comfortably.

### Which SEO tool should I use for local rankings?

For businesses that rely on Maps and local listings, compare tools by supported locations, local rank reports, and listing management. Track calls or bookings separately so you can connect visibility with business outcomes.

### Can one tool track Google rankings and AI visibility?

Some platforms, including Uplumo, offer both. Google rankings are positions for keywords in search results. AI visibility tracks mentions or citations observed in sampled answers.
