## Why teams look for a Profound alternative

![Reasons teams look past Profound: public price, small prompt set, fixes not reports, leads not scores](/site/blog/profound-alternatives-fit.webp)

*Match the reason you are switching to the alternative that solves it.*

- **No public price.** Profound's paid plan is custom. Smaller teams often want to start on a monthly plan they can cancel.
- **More capacity than needed.** A single brand can learn a lot from 15 to 125 well-chosen prompts.
- **Reports without next steps.** Some teams want audits, content help or recommendations next to the visibility data.
- **No link to revenue.** A visibility score does not show whether a citation produced a visit or a lead.

Profound remains a strong choice when you need enterprise reporting, many brands and broad engine coverage under one contract.

## How to switch without losing your baseline

1. **Export what you have.** Save your prompt list, engines, markets and the last few weeks of mention and citation rates.
2. **Recreate the same panel.** Use identical prompt text and the engines both tools share. Note any engine the new tool lacks.
3. **Run both for two weeks if you can.** Compare rates on the shared engines. Expect small differences from sampling.
4. **Keep the method written down.** Record how mentions, citations and share of voice are counted so reports stay comparable. Our guide to [tracking competitors in AI search](/blog/how-to-track-competitors-in-ai-search) shows a method you can reuse.
