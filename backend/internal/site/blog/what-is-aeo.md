**AEO (answer engine optimization) is the practice of shaping your content so answer engines can lift it as a direct answer and cite your page as the source.** Answer engines include Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, featured snippets, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and voice assistants. Classic SEO aims for a ranked link. AEO aims for the sentence inside the answer, plus the citation that points back to you.

Picture someone asking, “Can I cancel a hotel booking 48 hours before arrival?” One page buries the rule under a paragraph about company values. Another says, in its first line, “Flexible bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival.” The second page is the one an answer engine can quote. That is AEO in one example.

## How AEO works

An answer engine does three things when someone asks a question:

1. **Retrieves pages.** Google uses its normal search index. ChatGPT search uses `OAI-SearchBot` and partner results. Perplexity uses `PerplexityBot`. A page the crawler cannot fetch or index is out of the running.
2. **Picks passages.** It looks for text that answers the exact question, with the right scope, in a form it can trust and repeat.
3. **Writes and cites.** It composes an answer and links some of the pages it used. Which pages get linked varies by engine, question and time.

AEO works on step 2 and supports step 1. You cannot control step 3. You can make your page the clearest, best-supported passage available, and you can check which page each engine actually cites.

Google says its normal SEO guidance applies to AI Overviews and AI Mode, with no special markup or AI text file required. That makes AEO an extension of good SEO, not a replacement. [Google AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

## AEO vs SEO vs GEO

| | SEO | AEO | GEO |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Wins** | A ranked link and the click | The quoted answer to one question | Mentions, recommendations and citations across many AI answers |
| **Shows up in** | Google and Bing results | Featured snippets, People Also Ask, AI Overviews, voice | ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot |
| **Measured by** | Impressions, clicks, position | Which page wins the answer for each question | Mention rate and citation rate across a prompt panel |

The three overlap heavily. For the full comparison and a 30-day plan that covers all three, read [SEO vs AEO vs GEO](/blog/seo-vs-aeo-vs-geo).

## How to do AEO in five steps

![Five AEO steps: collect questions, answer first, add proof, keep it crawlable, re-check citations](/site/blog/what-is-aeo-steps.webp)

*Run this loop on one page at a time, starting with the page closest to a sale.*

### 1. Collect the questions people actually ask

Pull questions from sales calls, support tickets, Google Search Console queries, People Also Ask boxes and the prompts you test in AI assistants. Group them by page. A pricing page might own “How much does it cost?” and “Is there a free plan?”, while a policy page owns cancellation and refund questions.

### 2. Answer first, under a question heading

Use the question, or a close version of it, as an H2 or H3. Put a one or two sentence answer directly under it, then the detail. Keep the answer self-contained: it should still make sense if it is the only thing a reader sees.

| Weak opening | Answer-first opening |
| --- | --- |
| “Shipping times depend on many factors.” | “Standard orders ship within 2 business days and arrive in 3–5 days in the US.” |
| “We offer flexible pricing for teams.” | “Team plans cost a fixed monthly fee per workspace, with unlimited users.” |
| “Our clinic serves the wider area.” | “The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 8am–6pm, and accepts walk-ins before noon.” |

### 3. Add proof an engine can check

State scope, exceptions, units and dates next to the claim. Link the policy, show the screenshot, publish the test or dataset. Original evidence is the thing a competing summary page cannot copy, and it is what makes a passage worth citing.

### 4. Keep it crawlable and consistent

Serve the answer as HTML text, not only inside an image, a PDF or a script-rendered widget. Check that your CDN does not challenge search crawlers. Make sure directories, review sites and your own pages state the same facts. The [AI citation checklist](/blog/how-to-get-website-cited-by-ai) walks through each check.

### 5. Re-check the same questions

Save the questions, run them again in each engine over several days and record which URL each answer cites. One screenshot is an observation. A repeated panel shows a trend.

## Common AEO mistakes

- **Writing for a robot.** Repeating the question in every sentence or splitting text into tiny chunks does not help. Google says artificial chunking and special AI files are unnecessary.
- **Hiding the answer.** A long intro, an accordion that loads by script, or a key number only in an image all make the passage harder to use.
- **Answering without proof.** “Best in class” and “fast delivery” give an engine nothing to cite. Numbers, scope and dates do.
- **Measuring one screenshot.** Answers vary by run. Track a panel of questions over time before deciding anything changed.
- **Ignoring other sources.** If directories describe you differently from your site, an engine may repeat the directory. Fix listings you control.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does AEO stand for?

AEO stands for answer engine optimization. It means structuring content so search features and AI assistants can use it as a direct answer and cite the page.

### Is AEO the same as SEO?

No, but it builds on SEO. SEO earns a ranked link. AEO earns the quoted answer. A page usually needs to be crawlable and indexed, which is SEO work, before it can win an answer.

### Does schema markup help AEO?

Structured data helps engines understand what a page is about when it matches the visible content. It does not replace a clear, answer-first page. Google says no special AI schema is required for AI Overviews.

### How long does AEO take to work?

Google and AI assistants need to recrawl the page first, which can take days to weeks. Track the same questions for several weeks and compare cited URLs before and after the change.
