## Separate mentions citations and visits

Being named in an AI answer, receiving a website citation and getting a customer are separate outcomes.

| Result | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Brand mention | The answer names your business |
| Website citation | The answer includes your website as a linked source |
| Website visit or enquiry | Someone reaches your site or takes a tracked action |

A directory can be cited while your business is recommended in the text. Your article can also be cited without the answer recommending your product. These deserve different next steps.

## Test real buying questions

Searching only for your business name tells you little about discovery. Someone who already knows your name has already found you.

Build a small set of questions around real buying situations. For an illustrative hotel example:

- Which family resorts near [town] have a swimming pool?
- Where can I stay near [town] with activities for children?
- Which resorts within two hours of [city] suit a two-night family trip?

For software, include the buyer's use case, team size or budget where relevant. Use questions from sales calls, support requests and your own search data. Do not present an AI-generated prompt list as measured customer search volume.

## Inspect the sources behind recommendations

Read the linked pages instead of stopping at the list of names.

Suppose an answer cites a travel guide that clearly lists a competitor's family facilities. Your website describes itself as “the perfect destination,” but does not explain room occupancy, children's activities or pool access.

That comparison suggests a useful content gap. It does not prove you have uncovered the AI system's ranking formula.

For each missed question, keep a simple record:

| Question | Cited source | What to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A family stay with activities | A destination guide | Are your actual activities described clearly online? |
| A tool for a specific workflow | A comparison article | Does your product page explain that workflow with evidence? |
| A local service recommendation | A directory | Is your legitimate listing accurate and complete? |

Use **Brand Lookup** to research available brand or domain citation data, and saved tracker answers to inspect where competitors appear. Uplumo's **Cite Fix** can also reuse saved visibility evidence where available alongside its audit findings.

If a relevant independent source has inaccurate information about your business, request a factual correction. If you are missing from a legitimate directory, check its submission policy. Avoid buying mentions just to influence AI answers.

## Check crawler access

Useful information needs to be accessible before it can support an answer.

Review important pages for access blocks, error responses, snippet restrictions and content that is absent from the initial HTML. Check your hosting security rules as well as robots.txt; a bot can be allowed in one place and blocked in another.

OpenAI documents different controls for [OAI-SearchBot and GPTBot](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots). The former is used for search discovery; the latter relates to training. Allowing search access does not mean you must allow training access.

For Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, [Google's guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) says supporting pages must be indexed and eligible to display snippets. It does not require a special AI file or special schema.

Use the individual evidence to decide whether a finding matters for the page. A missing date, short page or absent schema is a review prompt, not proof that an assistant rejected your business.

## Improve the page buyers need

Choose one missed customer question and identify the page that should answer it. You may already have the right page; it might simply lack the details a buyer needs.

For example, “We offer memorable family experiences” leaves important questions open. A useful replacement might explain which activities are available, who can use them, any age limits and what costs extra. Use only details that are true for your business.

## Measure changes in answers and traffic

Save the original answer and record the URL, edit date and change you made. Revisit the same questions using comparable settings.

Review three things separately:

1. **Answers:** Is your business mentioned, and is your website cited?
2. **Visitors:** Are identifiable AI referrals reaching the updated page?
3. **Outcomes:** Are those visitors completing the lead events you configured?

If you appear more often but receive no visits, review whether the citations are clickable and which pages they point to. If visits arrive without enquiries, inspect the landing page and offer. If results vary, keep collecting comparable observations before claiming progress.

A before-and-after change gives you evidence to investigate. For ongoing monitoring, use a [fixed competitor prompt panel](/blog/how-to-track-competitors-in-ai-search) and keep site edits in your measurement notes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a tool guarantee that ChatGPT recommends my business?

No. Uplumo can help you observe answers, investigate gaps and improve your website. It cannot control which businesses an AI system recommends.

### Will a better Cite Fix score get me cited?

A better score means more of Uplumo's checks passed on the scanned pages. It is not a citation probability. Assess the suggested changes on their usefulness and track actual answers separately.

### Can Uplumo tell me the exact prompt every visitor used?

The prompts you track are questions you choose to test. Referral analytics records visits it can identify. Those are different datasets; do not assume a tracked question was the exact private prompt that produced a visit.
