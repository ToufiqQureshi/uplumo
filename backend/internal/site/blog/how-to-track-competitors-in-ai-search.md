You ask ChatGPT for the best booking software for an independent hotel. A competitor appears first. On a second run, it appears third. In another answer, a review site supplies the evidence and no company is presented as a winner. A screenshot of the first run is an observation. A repeatable panel of prompts tells you which competitors tend to appear and where they get their evidence.

**To check competitor rankings in AI search, track the same buyer questions across the same engines and compare brand mentions, owned-domain citations and explicit recommendations.** Keep Google rank tracking and keyword volume in separate columns. Use those figures to choose the questions worth monitoring.

## Choose the competitors and questions

Start with two to four companies your buyer might genuinely consider. Include one business that regularly appears in the answers even if it rarely ranks beside you in Google. Track review sites, directories and publishers as **sources**. Track them as **competitors** only if a buyer could choose them in place of your offer.

Create a short list of questions from sales calls, reviews, support, Search Console and keyword research. Give each prompt one intent and a stable ID.

| ID | Buyer stage | Example prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P01 | Discovery | What software helps an independent hotel take more direct bookings? |
| P02 | Comparison | Compare hotel booking engines that support deposits and promo codes. |
| P03 | Purchase | Which hotel booking engines fit a 30-room property that takes payments on its website? |
| P04 | Local or market fit | Which booking systems suit independent hotels in India that need UPI payments? |
| P05 | Brand accuracy | Does [brand] support deposits on direct bookings? Link to the official documentation. |

Start with 12 to 20 questions. Keep branded checks in a separate group, since mentioning a competitor by name in the prompt changes the task. Write down what counts as an alias for each brand and which domains count as first-party sources. Manually review ambiguous names, like a product name shared with a common word or an acquired company.

## Separate keyword volume from AI demand

The prompt “Which Lonavala resorts have indoor activities when it rains?” can matter to a hotel even if a keyword database has little data for that exact sentence. Group it under a relevant search theme such as “family resorts in Lonavala.” Use Google demand as context and record where each figure comes from.

| Measurement | Where to get it | What it tells you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Google keyword search volume** | Uplumo Keyword Research, Google Keyword Planner or another keyword provider | An estimate of search demand for a keyword in a selected market and time range. |
| **Google impressions and average position** | Search Console for your own website | Observed search exposure and average position for your property. Query coverage is subject to Search Console reporting limits. |
| **Google competitor position** | A SERP check or a rank tracker with the relevant domain, location and device | An observed placement in a specific set of results at a point in time. |
| **Estimated AI prompt demand** | A tool with a published methodology, if available for the question | A directional estimate. For example, Ahrefs bases its adjusted figure on parent-keyword Google volume and estimated platform usage. |
| **Sampled AI visibility** | Uplumo AI Visibility or a manually recorded panel | How often a brand or its domain appeared in the answers you checked. |

*Keep provider estimates and first-party observations labelled. A sampled mention rate measures the panel you ran.*

To build the Google side of the benchmark in Uplumo, open **Keyword Research**, choose your target market and look up the parent keyword. For results with a competitor snapshot, inspect the sites shown above your result. Use a SERP check to record a specific rival’s URL and position, including cases where your own site is absent.

Avoid adding overlapping volumes for near-identical keywords and calling the result the total size of a market. When a provider shows missing data, record it as unavailable. Prioritise questions using search demand, buyer relevance and your ability to answer with proof. A low-volume question about an important deal blocker can deserve a place on your panel.

For manual cross-checks, use fresh conversations and keep the account, web-search setting and other test conditions as consistent as possible. Separate app results from API or provider results. If a check fails, record it. If the engine returns a valid answer with no sources, keep that observation and record zero citations. Never silently discard an answer because it leaves your brand out.

### Calculate rates you can explain

Say you checked ten questions across two engines on two dates. You attempted 40 runs. Two failed, leaving 38 valid answers. These illustrative numbers use one row per answer and count a brand or domain at most once per answer.

| Metric | Example | Calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Check coverage** | 38 valid answers out of 40 attempts | **95%** |
| **Your brand mention rate** | Named in 9 of 38 valid answers | **24%** rounded |
| **Competitor mention rate** | Named in 15 of 38 valid answers | **39%** rounded |
| **Your owned-domain citation rate** | Linked in 4 of 38 valid answers | **11%** rounded |
| **Competitor owned-domain citation rate** | Linked in 8 of 38 valid answers | **21%** rounded |

Two brands can appear in the same answer, so their mention rates can add up to more than 100%. If you use share of voice, state the denominator. For example, the brand’s mention counts divided by the sum for a fixed list of tracked brands answers a different question from the percentage of answers naming that brand. If your tool reports an average position, use it only with its documented definition. In a manual panel, record placement only in an explicitly ordered recommendation list and report how often such a list appeared.

### Compare equivalent samples

Review each engine and market before merging results into one dashboard. If a second engine was added this month, compare the common engines separately. If web-enabled answers declined sharply, report that beside citation rates. A shift from three to four mentions in a small sample is a prompt to inspect the underlying answers. It takes repeated evidence to decide whether a competitor gained meaningful ground.

## Find the sources driving competitor visibility

A rival can appear often because directories describe it clearly, because its own pages explain a particular feature, or because the question names the product. The next step depends on which evidence you see.

![Competitor citation evidence matrix showing when to improve your page, correct listings or check your positioning](/site/blog/ai-competitor-tracking-source-gap.webp)

*Trace the answer back to the cited passage. Use the source type and the claim to choose the next action.*

| Pattern | What to inspect | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rival appears with links to its own documentation | The exact feature, setup detail or limitation the page answers | Improve your equivalent page if your product can meet the same need. |
| Rival appears with links to a directory or comparison | The listing attributes and the publisher’s inclusion policy | Correct factual gaps in your listing or submit evidence to the publisher through its stated process. |
| Rival appears in Google and AI answers, but your offer differs | The buyer intent and the trade-off the answer values | Explain who your offer serves, when it fits and what limitations apply. |
| Your site is linked but your brand is absent from the answer | Whether the cited page is an educational guide or a product page | Keep the guide useful. Add a clear connection to the relevant service or product where it helps the reader. |

For each meaningful gap, log the question ID, engine, rival, cited domain, exact URL, the claim it supports and the page you can improve. Keep both the original cited URL and a normalised domain or preferred URL for grouping. Store meaningful query parameters where they distinguish content. This lets you see whether one rival page, several pages or a third-party source accounts for the gap.

## Build a weekly tracking routine

| Cadence | What to check | Decision it supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily when needed** | Site availability, tracked leads and sudden changes in identifiable referrals. Use daily rank or prompt checks when your tool and campaign warrant them. | Catch tracking or conversion problems while they are actionable. |
| **Weekly** | Fixed AI prompt panel, citation sources and Google keyword positions in the same location and device settings. Uplumo’s scheduled Google rank checks run weekly. | Identify which rival gained ground for a question and which URL to improve. |
| **Monthly** | Search demand estimates, new competitor domains and the enquiries or signups tied to relevant landing pages. | Refresh exploratory questions while retaining a stable set for trends. |

In Uplumo, pair **Rank Tracking** and **Keyword Research** with the saved AI panel. Use Google Search Console to understand discovery for your own site, and Uplumo Analytics or GA4 for identifiable visits and configured outcomes. The result should be a short list of decisions, such as “publish the payment compatibility table for P04” or “check why the comparison page gets referrals but no completed demos.”

### Can I check a competitor’s exact ChatGPT search volume?

Tools may provide estimates, coverage counts or other demand signals. Read each methodology and state the scope. You can measure how often the competitor appears in a defined panel and use market-specific Google keyword estimates as a separate clue about topic demand.

### Can I track every competitor with one prompt?

A category prompt can surface useful rivals. Build a panel of multiple buyer questions so you can distinguish a broad mention from a company that repeatedly appears for one service, market or buying requirement.

### Which AI competitor metrics should I report to clients?

Start with panel size, checks that completed, mention rate, owned-domain citation rate and the leading cited pages. Add Google ranking changes and identifiable leads where relevant, with a date range and market. Show one example answer that supports each decision.
