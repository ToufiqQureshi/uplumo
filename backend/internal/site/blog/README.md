# Blog content removed

The owner explicitly requested removal of every blog on 30 October 2026.
There are no published article sources. README.md is developer documentation
and is excluded by the loader. Article content and blog-only SVG assets were
removed, along with the previous 29 redirects. All former article URLs and the
empty /blog index return 404. Public navigation and sitemap omit the blog.

The generic Markdown renderer remains for compatibility and is tested with
isolated fixtures. Do not restore old posts without a new owner request.
Product screenshots are shared product assets and remain separate from blogs.
