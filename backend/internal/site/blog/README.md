# Uplumo blog content

The owner approved two new practical guides on 8 November 2026: Google ranking diagnosis and AI citation gaps. Posts use the existing Markdown renderer and appear in the blog index and sitemap.

Use title, description and date front matter; the filename is the URL slug. The post template supplies the H1. Keep article body headings at H2 and below. Titles are limited to 70 characters and descriptions to 50–160 characters. Product claims must match the implementation; do not promise rankings or citations.

The previous article collection and redirects remain retired. Do not restore those URLs without a new owner request. Shared product screenshots remain separate from blogs.
