## One platform for the work around a search opportunity

A search opportunity becomes valuable when a team acts on it. Someone must understand the customer question, decide which page should answer it, improve the content, inspect technical access and review the outcome.

Uplumo combines tools for those stages. Keyword and SERP research informs the page assignment. Search briefs support planning. The content editor provides SEO and AEO checks plus AI writing assistance. Site audit supplies technical findings. AI visibility tools help the team inspect observed answers, while analytics and connected Google data support measurement.

That is the product's practical promise: give people a coherent place to investigate and improve their presence in search and AI answer experiences.

## AI Visibility: Tracker, Brand Lookup and Prompt Explorer

The **AI Visibility Tracker** saves a question set, selected engines and market settings so repeat checks can produce comparable history. It preserves observed answers and sources. Collection methods differ: some engines use queued consumer-site collection, while others use APIs. Read those labels and the successful-answer coverage before comparing rates.

**Brand Lookup** researches mentions and sources from a provider dataset. It can show brands appearing alongside you, cited pages and available sampled questions. It is not a census of every conversation, and its ChatGPT dataset has a US-English boundary.

**Prompt Explorer** lets you inspect selected model responses to a chosen question, with available citations and search context. For example, an accountant can study a service-selection question, open a cited source and check what the relevant service page explains. Country and model support vary. These observed API/provider responses can differ from a consumer app.

## Cite Fix: turn a page signal into an improvement

Cite Fix scores stored signals from the latest completed site audit, lists affected pages and prioritises checks. It can combine those observations with available saved AI answers, sampled rival-page evidence, topic clusters and citation history. The score summarises rules; it cannot establish the private reason an assistant chose a source.

## Site Audit and Rank Tracking: technical access and search positions

**Site Audit** crawls reachable pages within its scope, reviews technical and content signals, and supports comparisons between completed scans. Optional JavaScript rendering applies to eligible flagged shells after explicit approval. It complements Google's URL Inspection rather than replacing Google's indexed-state evidence. See the [audit guide](/blog/uplumo-advanced-site-audit-crawler).

**Rank Tracking** saves keywords, location, devices and depth, and records available positions, ranking URLs and history. Weekly scheduling is the default advertised option; changing schedule, depth or devices changes usage. A rank is a sampled observation under that configuration. “Outside depth” does not mean absent from Google's entire index. Check the cost estimate and shared credit forecast before enabling a larger workload.

## Keyword Research, Saved Keywords and competitor research

**Keyword Research** uses a conversational agent to investigate an offer, audience and market with controlled data tools. Available volume, difficulty, intent and result-feature evidence can guide a page assignment, but coverage differs by source and market. Tell the agent what the business actually sells before selecting an opportunity.

**Saved Keywords** retains selected terms and available metrics for filtering, tagging and export. It helps turn discovery into a maintained shortlist rather than a new research session every time. The [keyword workflow guide](/blog/uplumo-ai-keyword-research-content-workflow) shows the handoff to a brief.

**Domain Overview** provides a competitor-domain research view of its estimated organic footprint and available competitor evidence. **Backlinks** explores links and referring domains. These are provider datasets, not access to a competitor's private analytics. Open source pages and review freshness, relevance and coverage before deciding what to pursue. See the [backlink research guide](/blog/ethical-backlinks-for-small-business).

## Content Editor, AI writing and publishing

The **Content Editor** combines a draft with search briefs and inspectable SEO/AEO feedback. **AI writing** can help outline, answer first, fill gaps or revise selected content. A human reviewer verifies the resulting claims, sources and original value. A high score is not a Google ranking forecast.

For an eligible plan and connected site, **WordPress publishing** sends the reviewed content to that site. Confirm the destination and inspect the public result. **IndexNow** notifies participating engines such as Bing about changed URLs; it is not Google's indexing service. Read the [writing and publishing guide](/blog/uplumo-ai-content-writing-search-briefs) and [scoring guide](/blog/uplumo-seo-aeo-content-scoring).

## Analytics and Search Performance: review visits and search activity

**Uplumo Analytics** uses the installed tracker to record available visits, recognised sources and configured interactions. Known assistant referrers or campaign tags can identify AI referrals; missing source information cannot be magically recovered. Optional returning-browser recognition has separate storage and consent implications.

**Search Performance** and connected **GA4** views use authorised Google properties on eligible plans. Search impressions and clicks, analytics sessions and verified enquiries are distinct measurements. Review [how to reconcile them](/blog/uplumo-ai-traffic-analytics-search-performance) before reporting growth.

## Reports, SAM, Context and Agent setup

**Reports** turns selected project evidence into a reviewable output. Label the date range, collection method, missing data and next actions. Business-plan white-label options let an eligible workspace apply its own report branding. Treat a shared report link as a sharing decision and check its contents before distribution.

**SAM assistant** is a beta assistant for working with project context and available tools. Ask a narrow question and inspect the evidence behind its response. It does not remove the need for judgement, and permitted paid actions can consume the same shared credits as work elsewhere in the app.

**Context** records the business's offer and positioning for project work. Supply accurate audience, service and competitor details; inaccurate context can produce irrelevant suggestions. **Agent setup** provides MCP access for supported external AI clients. An API key is a credential: connect it only to a client you trust. Agent access follows tool availability, workspace permissions and usage controls; it is not unlimited free research.

## A practical SEO client report template

Use one row per important page or question group so the client can trace the work:

| Report field | What to include |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Project, market, devices, dates and collection method |
| Baseline | Comparable search, answer or recorded-visit evidence |
| Finding | Affected URL and the observation supporting the issue |
| Work completed | Specific edit or technical repair, owner and date |
| Verification | Follow-up crawl, inspected public page or saved evidence |
| Outcome | Separate impressions, clicks, visits, enquiries and confirmed sales |
| Next action | The next test, missing evidence and review date |

Do not label an enquiry-button click as a sale or a score increase as an earned citation. If a connection is missing or the period has too few observations, state that limitation. A useful client report explains what changed and what is still unknown, rather than decorating a page with unrelated totals.

## Research that gives a writer direction

Uplumo's keyword tools support suggestions, ideas, related queries and SERP review. Where available, intent, difficulty and other metrics add context. The content brief brings search evidence closer to production through terms, questions, subtopics and available competitor pages.

A useful brief tells the writer what the reader needs and where original business knowledge belongs. For example, a product comparison page should explain real differences and fit criteria. A troubleshooting guide should show steps, expected observations and exceptions.

Uplumo supports both by giving the team a research foundation and a place to develop the answer. The [research workflow](/blog/uplumo-ai-keyword-research-content-workflow) explains the handoff from a query to an article assignment.

## Content intelligence with visible checks

The editor's SEO and AEO modes address different editorial questions. SEO feedback can review topical coverage, placement and structure. AEO feedback can review a direct opening answer, relevant questions, supporting sources, authorship and freshness.

These checks make revision easier to discuss. Instead of asking for “more optimization,” a reviewer can ask for a missing explanation, a clearer answer or evidence for a factual statement. AI writing actions support those specific changes.

That combination is useful across team sizes. One owner can use it to structure a revision; a larger team can use it to make editorial expectations more consistent.

## Auditing and AI visibility support the same project

Uplumo's crawler inspects reachable pages within the chosen scope and produces page-level and site-wide findings. Audit history comparisons help reviewers see changes between completed runs. AI-related checks add visibility into selected access and business-clarity signals.

The AI visibility tools answer a different set of questions: what was observed in an answer, which sources were available and which brands appeared? Together, these workflows help the team investigate technical and content evidence without conflating them.

A crawler finding is an observable issue to review. An answer sample is an observation from a platform and context. Use both to make a better decision about the actual page.

## A complete weekly operating loop

A practical team routine is:

1. Select one important search or customer question.
2. Review research and current results.
3. Improve the relevant page from a grounded brief.
4. Check technical findings and internal paths.
5. Inspect a stable set of AI questions where appropriate.
6. Review measured visits and verified business outcomes.

For a hypothetical B2B team, that project might be a comparison page leading to a demo request. The team checks the page's evidence, revises the explanation, reviews crawl access and tracks whether relevant enquiries follow. No invented conversion uplift is needed to make the process useful.

Google's [SEO guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) and [generative AI Search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) provide useful context for the fundamentals. Uplumo supplies tools for applying a disciplined workflow around them.

## FAQ

**Is Uplumo positioned only for small businesses?**

No. SMBs through enterprise marketing teams can evaluate its workflow, with the project scope matched to current capabilities, capacity and organizational requirements.

**Does the platform automatically guarantee rankings or citations?**

Search engines and assistants decide what to surface. Uplumo supports research, improvement and measurement so the team can work from evidence.
