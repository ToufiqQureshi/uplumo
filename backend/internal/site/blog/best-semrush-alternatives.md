Semrush is a strong all-round SEO suite, but its paid plans start at $139 a month and AI prompt tracking needs the $199 Starter plan. If you mainly need rank tracking, backlink data or a simpler workflow, you may pay for tools you never open.

**The best Semrush alternatives, by what you need most:**

- **Ahrefs**: best for backlink research and competitor analysis, with an AI Brand Radar add-on.
- **SE Ranking**: best value for daily rank tracking, with 2,000 keywords on its entry plan.
- **Moz Pro**: best lower-cost entry plan at $99 a month, with clear metrics for learning SEO.
- **Ubersuggest**: best for solo owners who want simple keyword ideas and site checks.
- **Uplumo**: best for one workspace that joins Google data, AI answers and confirmed leads, with a free plan.

## What the entry plans cost

![Bar chart of entry SEO plan prices: Moz Pro $99, Ahrefs Lite $129, SE Ranking Essential $129, Semrush SEO $139, Semrush Starter $199](/site/blog/semrush-alternatives-prices.webp)

*Published list prices in USD, billed monthly. Keyword limits differ, so compare the cost of the keywords you actually track.*

Divide the price by the keywords you need to track. SE Ranking Essential tracks 2,000 keywords daily for $129, about 6 cents a keyword. Semrush's SEO plan tracks 500 for $139, about 28 cents. If daily rank tracking is your main job, that gap matters more than the headline price.

## Each alternative in detail

### Ahrefs

Ahrefs is known for its backlink index and Site Explorer. The Lite plan ($129) covers 5 non-verified projects and 750 tracked keywords. Standard ($249) raises that to 2,000 keywords. The Brand Radar add-on, from $199 a month, adds AI visibility research across a large prompt database. Ahrefs also offers a free tier with limited access to Site Explorer and Site Audit.

**Choose it when** link research and competitor content gaps drive your work.

### SE Ranking

SE Ranking's Essential plan ($129) tracks 2,000 keywords daily, and Pro ($279) tracks 5,000. Its AI visibility add-on starts at $71 a month for 75 prompts across Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, ChatGPT and Copilot. A 14-day trial includes starter limits for both rank tracking and AI prompts.

**Choose it when** you track many keywords or clients and want AI tracking as an add-on, not a new tool.

### Moz Pro

Moz Pro Standard costs $99 a month for 3 tracked sites and 300 tracked keywords. Medium ($179) covers 10 sites and 1,500 keywords. Every plan includes MozBar Premium and link research. AI answer tracking is not part of the published plans.

**Choose it when** you want a lower entry price and well-known metrics such as Domain Authority.

### Ubersuggest

Ubersuggest focuses on keyword ideas, site audits and rank tracking with a simpler interface. Its pricing page lists plan tiers and limits. Check whether the plan you want tracks rankings daily or weekly before you buy.

**Choose it when** you run one site and want straightforward keyword research.

### Uplumo

Uplumo joins the data most small teams check separately. Connect Google Search Console and GA4, run site audits, write in the content editor, track keywords weekly, and see how ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity answer your buyers' questions. Uplumo Analytics records AI referrals and the lead events you configure, so you can see whether a ranking or citation produced an enquiry. The free plan includes analytics, a starter audit and the content editor. Paid research, AI checks and scheduled checks use workspace credits.

**Choose it when** you want to see Google rankings, AI answers and leads together, and you do not need daily rank tracking.

## How to choose

![Pick by the job: Ahrefs for backlinks, SE Ranking for daily rank tracking, Moz Pro for learning, Uplumo for Google, AI and leads](/site/blog/semrush-alternatives-choose.webp)

*Start from the job you repeat every week, not the longest feature list.*

1. **List your weekly jobs.** Rank checks, link research, audits, content briefs, AI answer checks or client reports.
2. **Count your volume.** Keywords tracked, sites, users and AI prompts. Compare plans on those numbers.
3. **Run the same test in each trial.** Use five keywords, one competitor domain and three buyer questions. Compare the data and how quickly you reach a decision.
4. **Check the AI side.** If AI answers matter to your buyers, decide whether you need prompt tracking now. Our [best AEO tools comparison](/blog/best-aeo-tools) covers specialist options.

If your site is not ranking at all, no tool switch will fix it. Start with [why a website is not ranking on Google](/blog/why-website-not-ranking-google).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the cheapest alternative to Semrush?

For paid SEO suites, Moz Pro Standard is the lowest entry price in this comparison at $99 a month. Ahrefs also sells a limited Starter plan at $29. Uplumo has a free plan with analytics, a starter audit and the content editor.

### Is Ahrefs better than Semrush?

Neither is better for everyone. Ahrefs is usually preferred for backlink research. Semrush covers more marketing channels in one suite. Compare them on the data you use every week.

### Which Semrush alternative tracks AI search?

SE Ranking sells an AI visibility add-on, Ahrefs sells Brand Radar, and Uplumo includes Prompt Explorer and saved AI checks on its Pro plan. Moz Pro's published plans do not include AI answer tracking.
