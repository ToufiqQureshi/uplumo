**SEO (search engine optimization) gets your pages ranked in Google and Bing results. AEO (answer engine optimization) shapes a page so a search feature or assistant can lift a direct answer from it. GEO (generative engine optimization) works on how AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity describe, recommend and cite your business.** All three start from the same public, crawlable page. They differ in where you appear and how you measure success.

Here is how that looks for one business. A mountain resort can rank on Google for “family resorts near [town]” (SEO), have its cancellation rule quoted for “Can I cancel a family package 48 hours before?” (AEO) and still watch ChatGPT recommend three competitors for “Where should I stay in the mountains with kids during the rainy season?” (GEO). Those are three different outcomes, so track rankings, answers, citations and website visits separately and see which pages actually bring enquiries or bookings.

## SEO vs AEO vs GEO at a glance

| | SEO | AEO | GEO |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Goal** | Rank a page in search results and earn the click | Be the clear, quotable answer to a specific question | Be mentioned, recommended and cited inside AI-generated answers |
| **Where you show up** | Google and Bing organic results, Google Maps | Featured snippets, People Also Ask, Google AI Overviews, voice answers | ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Google AI Mode |
| **What helps most** | Crawlable pages, search intent, internal links, useful content, earned links | Answer-first paragraphs, question headings, tables, steps and accurate facts in visible text | Verifiable facts, original evidence, consistent listings and third-party sources that describe you correctly |
| **How you measure it** | Impressions, clicks and average position in Search Console, plus rank tracking | Which pages win snippets and AI Overview links for your questions | Mention rate, citation rate and recommended competitors in a fixed prompt panel, plus AI referral visits |
| **Typical first fix** | Get the money pages indexed and matched to the query | Answer the main question in the first two sentences of the page | Find the pages AI cites for your buyers' questions and fix what your page leaves vague |

For a deeper look at the middle column, read [what AEO is](/blog/what-is-aeo). For the GEO side in ChatGPT specifically, see [how to rank in ChatGPT](/blog/how-to-rank-in-chatgpt).

Most of the work overlaps. A page that Google can index, that answers the question in its opening lines and that states verifiable details in plain text serves all three. The difference is mostly in **measurement**: a Google ranking, an answer box and an AI citation are separate signals, and one can move while the others stay flat.

## Get your pages indexed on Google

Start with the URLs that can make money for the business. For a hotel, that might be room, package and direct-booking pages. For a SaaS company, it might be pricing, integrations and use-case pages. A site audit helps you find technical problems. **Google Search Console’s URL Inspection tool tells you how Google sees a specific URL.** Use both when investigating an important page.

![Illustrated workflow from publishing an accessible URL through discovery, URL Inspection and an indexing request](/site/blog/best-seo-aeo-geo-tools-google-indexing.webp)

*Publish the preferred URL, give Google a route to discover it, then inspect the live page.*

### Before you request indexing

1. **Open the live URL on mobile.** Confirm the page is public, returns HTTP `200` and shows the main text and links without a login. Check whether your firewall is serving a bot challenge or your site is sending a `403`, `429` or `5xx`.
2. **Check the indexing rules.** Look for an unintended `noindex` tag or HTTP header. Check `robots.txt` for blocked paths. For a unique page you want indexed, make sure its canonical points to the preferred live URL.
3. **Give the page a way to be found.** Link to it from a related page people already use. Add the preferred URL to your XML sitemap and submit that sitemap in Search Console. Update the sitemap’s `lastmod` date when the page changes meaningfully.
4. **Inspect the exact URL.** Run **Test live URL** in Search Console and check the rendered page if the content depends on JavaScript. If the page is eligible, use **Request indexing** for a new or substantially updated priority page.
5. **Follow up in the Page indexing report.** Repeated requests for the same URL do not speed up crawling. Google says a crawl can take a few days to a few weeks, and it decides whether to index the page.

| What Search Console shows | What to investigate first |
| --- | --- |
| **Discovered – currently not indexed** | Check whether relevant pages link to the URL. Look for crawl barriers or intermittent server errors if the issue affects many URLs. |
| **Crawled – currently not indexed** | Check for near-duplicate pages and weak differentiation. Compare the page with the URL you actually want to rank and improve its distinct value if needed. |
| **Duplicate, Google chose different canonical than user** | Compare Google’s selected URL with your preferred URL. Align internal links, sitemap entries and canonical signals. |
| **Indexed, with little search traffic** | Review the queries and pages in Search Console. Check whether the page format, offer and title meet the intent shown in the results. |

**Can you ping Google for instant indexing?** For normal articles, service pages and landing pages, use Search Console and a sitemap. Google’s Indexing API is limited to eligible job postings and livestream pages. Uplumo’s paid plans support IndexNow submissions to Bing and other participating engines. Google discovery uses its own process.

## Give AI search crawlers access

Review crawler access according to what you want each platform to do. OpenAI and Anthropic document separate bots for search discovery, user-requested retrieval and model development. A team can allow search discovery while setting a separate policy for training crawlers.

| Crawler | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Googlebot** | Crawls pages for Google Search. Google’s AI features in Search use Googlebot crawling controls. |
| **OAI-SearchBot** | Helps OpenAI discover pages for ChatGPT search features. |
| **GPTBot** | Crawls content that may be used to train OpenAI’s foundation models. |
| **Claude-SearchBot** | Crawls content to improve Claude’s search results. |
| **ClaudeBot** | Crawls content that may be used for Anthropic’s model development. |

Check your existing `robots.txt` and review the rules for each relevant agent. If you use a CDN, firewall or bot protection service, review its request logs and bot rules as well. A crawler your file allows may still receive a challenge or error. Use the providers’ verification guidance where available before creating allow rules.

For example, if your crawler policy allows search discovery and your existing configuration blocks these two search agents, review their groups before changing the file. On a site where all public URLs may be crawled, the rules can look like this:

```text
User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
Allow: /

User-agent: Claude-SearchBot
Allow: /
```

Specific user-agent groups can change which rules apply to a crawler. Preserve any path restrictions each agent should follow, and keep genuinely private pages behind authentication.

Make the public page understandable once it can be fetched. State the business name, offer, location or market and relevant conditions in visible HTML. Put product specs, prices where appropriate, policies and service areas in text. Use structured data that matches what people see on the page. For Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Google says its normal SEO guidance applies, with no special AI markup or new text file required. For ordinary public pages, crawler access supports discovery. Inclusion in any generated answer is up to the platform and can vary by prompt.

## Write content for search intent

Start with a real question from sales calls, support tickets, Google Search Console or your AI prompt checks. Search it in your target market and look at the pages that already show up. Are people looking for a quick explanation, a shortlist, a location, a quote or something they can book? Choose the page format from the answer.

![Four content brief cards showing a buyer’s question, the matching page, original proof and a booking action](/site/blog/best-seo-aeo-geo-tools-content-brief.webp)

*An example content brief for a mountain resort serving families in the rainy season.*

| What someone asks | Page to create or improve | What they need to decide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How do I choose a mountain resort in the rainy season? | Practical guide | Driving conditions, indoor activities, age restrictions and seasonal trade-offs |
| Which resorts near [town] work for families with young children? | Comparison guide or family-stay landing page | Room occupancy, kids’ meals, indoor play and access to medical facilities |
| Can I book a family package for this weekend? | Offer or booking page | Inclusions, availability, price or a quote process and cancellation terms |
| Does this resort offer a day picnic for groups? | Dedicated service page | Group minimum, meal options, timings, transport and enquiry steps |

### Turn the question into a useful draft in Uplumo

1. **Pick a primary intent and a business outcome.** Use keyword research and the Google results to choose the page type. Decide whether the reader should check availability, compare plans or ask for a quote.
2. **Create a search brief in the Content Editor.** Review what top-ranking pages cover. Then add the details your business can prove, such as actual photos, original examples, check-in rules, shipping cut-offs or a clear breakdown of what’s included.
3. **Answer the main question early.** Use the topic naturally in the title, H1 and opening. Give each subheading a job. A heading like **What’s included in the family package?** helps a reader find what they came for.
4. **Add visuals where they answer a question.** Use actual product screenshots, venue photos or a process diagram. Give the image useful alt text and put important facts in the surrounding copy.
5. **Review focused suggestions and publish.** The editor’s SEO and AI-answer scores flag areas to review. Check every factual claim, add relevant internal links and use a clear next action. Paid plans can publish to a connected WordPress site.

For the family-stay example, a useful SEO title is **Family Resort in [Town] with Indoor Activities | [Resort]**. Keep the URL short and stable, such as `/family-stay/`. Write a description that tells readers what the page contains, such as room occupancy, indoor activities, family packages and booking terms. Use those details only where the business actually offers them.

For example, “Enjoy a perfect rainy-season escape” gives a family little to work with. A useful paragraph would read: “For a family stay in the rainy season, start by checking whether your room fits two adults and two children, which indoor activities run during heavy rain and whether meals are included. The package details below list occupancy, indoor facilities and cancellation terms.” Fill those sections with your actual policies and put the booking or enquiry option next to them.

## Check your AI visibility

Checking “What is [your brand]?” can reveal outdated descriptions, but it tells you little about whether a buyer would discover you. Start with unbranded questions that sound like something a customer would type, then add a branded question to test accuracy.

- **Discovery** — “Which mountain resorts suit families during the rainy season?”
- **Comparison** — “Compare family resorts near [town] with indoor activities and direct booking.”
- **Decision** — “Where can I book a weekend family package near [town] with meals included?”
- **Brand accuracy** — “What does [resort name] include in its family package, and what’s the official booking site?”

### Set up a lead or signup you can trust

1. Install the Uplumo tracking snippet on the pages where the journey happens. Open the site and confirm that page visits appear.
2. Have your site fire the event after the backend confirms the outcome. For example, call `window.uplumo("SignupCompleted")` after account creation succeeds or `window.uplumo("LeadSubmitted")` after an enquiry is accepted. A click on “Start free,” “Call” or “Book now” can be recorded as its own event.
3. Select the successful event in the **What counts as a lead?** section in Uplumo Analytics. Test a successful submission, a failed submission and a refresh of the confirmation page. Check that success is counted once and that a failed attempt adds no lead.
4. If you use GA4, configure the matching `sign_up`, `generate_lead` or other appropriate event, mark the outcome as a key event and test it in DebugView. If a booking happens in a separate engine, confirm that you can measure success there or reconcile the result with booking records.

A practical place to start in Search Console is a relevant query with meaningful impressions and an average position around 5–20. Open the **Pages** tab for that query to see which URL Google shows. If it already answers the intent, improve its title or add the missing details. If the wrong page ranks, check what it covers and how your internal links point to each page.

For a **daily check**, review high-value landing pages, recognised referrals, completed leads and sudden tracking or availability issues. For a **weekly check**, compare keyword positions, selected AI prompts and seven-day traffic and lead totals against a comparable period. If visits hold steady while leads drop, test the form or booking flow before rewriting the page. If AI mentions rise while identifiable visits stay flat, inspect whether the sampled answers link to a page with a useful next step.

## Improve your local SEO

For a hotel, clinic, restaurant or service business, customers may find you in Google Maps and call before they ever visit your website. **Google Business Profile**, formerly Google My Business, gives you a separate view of how people discover and interact with an eligible verified listing. Google says local results primarily depend on relevance, distance and prominence.

1. **Get the listing right.** Verify eligible locations, choose an accurate primary category, keep hours and contact details current, and add recent photos. Link each listing to its relevant location or service page.
2. **Make the location page worth opening.** Include real directions, parking, services, hours, photos and a contact or booking option. For a service-area business, explain which neighbourhoods you serve and what customers can book.
3. **Use Uplumo for local research.** Check location-specific search demand, compare the sites showing up for your service queries, and review sampled AI answers for competitor listings and links to Google Maps or review sites. Check first-party listing performance such as website clicks, calls and direction requests in the verified Google Business Profile.
4. **Connect the listing to the website journey.** Add a campaign tag to the profile’s website link where appropriate. Review traffic to the location page and configured enquiry or booking events. Compare them with Google’s profile interactions and business records. A call-button click measures the tap. Your call or CRM records tell you whether it became an enquiry.

A resort might see direction requests rise while direct bookings stay flat. If the listing is sending guests to the homepage, test a relevant landing page for the offer people are searching for. Use profile activity to understand local discovery, then use website and booking records to see what happens after the visit.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is AEO replacing SEO?

No. AI Overviews, ChatGPT search and Perplexity still retrieve pages from the web, and Google says its normal SEO guidance applies to its AI features. AEO and GEO add new places to appear and new things to measure. A page that cannot be crawled or indexed rarely shows up in any of them.

### What is the difference between AEO and GEO?

AEO is about being the direct answer to one question, such as a featured snippet or a quoted line in an AI Overview. GEO is about how a generative assistant describes, compares and recommends your business across many questions, and which sources it cites when it does. In practice the same page improvements support both. GEO adds monitoring of mentions, citations and competitors.

### How can I get a new blog indexed faster?

Make the page public and eligible, link to it from a relevant page, include its preferred URL in the sitemap and request indexing in Search Console for a priority URL. Fix any crawl or canonical problem you find. Google decides when to crawl it and whether to index it.

### Does allowing AI crawlers get my website cited in ChatGPT or Claude?

Allowing search crawlers helps the platform access your public content. Citation decisions vary by question and platform. Improve the page with specific, verifiable answers and monitor the prompts that matter to your business over time.

### Should I check SEO rankings daily?

Daily rank data is useful during a migration or when you need to monitor a competitive campaign closely. For routine work, review traffic, tracking and site availability daily if needed, then assess rankings and content progress over a longer window. Uplumo’s scheduled keyword checks run weekly.
