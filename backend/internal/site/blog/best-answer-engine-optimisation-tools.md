## What Uplumo does in practice

Say you run a software business and a customer asks, “Which booking systems let a small hotel take payments on its own website?” A useful AEO workflow starts with that question and ends with a decision about a real page.

1. **Capture the evidence.** Use **Brand Lookup** for available category data. In **Prompt Explorer**, run the question across supported models. Save important questions for repeat checks and record the cited URLs. An answer may mention your company while linking to a third-party directory. That calls for a different fix than a citation to your own page.
2. **Find the missing detail.** Read the rival pages cited for the same question. If they explain payment methods, supported markets and deposit rules while your site says “seamless payments,” you have something concrete to investigate. Run a **Site Audit** and review **Cite Fix** where available to prioritise technical and content improvements.
3. **Improve the page buyers will use.** Bring the question into the **Content Editor**. Use the search brief to understand what pages already cover, then add verified product details, original screenshots or a worked example. The editor’s scores help review the draft. A paid plan can publish to a connected WordPress site.
4. **Check the outcome.** Recheck the saved questions. Review recognised AI referrals and their landing pages in **Uplumo Analytics**, and compare configured lead or signup events. Connect Search Console and GA4 where eligible to review Google discovery and website outcomes in the same workspace.

## How to test an AEO tool before buying

Use the same trial inputs across tools. If one vendor tests a broad branded prompt and another tests a narrow unbranded one, their visibility percentages tell you very little about relative coverage.

| Trial task | What to write down |
| --- | --- |
| Choose 12 questions a real customer could ask | Four discovery questions, four comparisons and four purchase or shortlist questions. Use one market and the engines the tools have in common. |
| Compare two business competitors | Record the exact answer, whether each business was named and which sites it linked. Check a common alias or product name manually. |
| Follow three citations to their pages | Record whether the links point to the vendor, a directory, a review or another kind of source. Check whether the page actually supports the claim. |
| Turn one gap into an edit | Pick the URL, the exact fact to add, the source of that fact and the person responsible. |
| Re-run the same set | Keep the prompt, engine and available location settings stable. Record changes in the answer and source alongside any change in measured visits. |

During the trial, ask how a tool handles failed checks, unanswered prompts and answers that use no web sources. Ask whether it shows the original response so you can audit the classification. If a vendor reports **prompt volume**, read the method. Google keyword volume, a modelled AI demand estimate and the number of answers in a sampled dataset are different measurements.

### Can an AEO tool get my website ranked first in ChatGPT?

It can show which sources appeared and help you find improvements. Answers can change by prompt, platform and time. Track whether buyers’ questions produce useful mentions and citations, then measure the visits and outcomes you can identify.

### Is an AI mention the same as an AI referral?

A mention is text in an answer. A citation is a linked source. A referral is a visit recorded with an identifiable source signal. An answer can mention your brand without linking to your site, and a link can appear without anyone clicking it.

### Do small businesses need an enterprise AEO platform?

Choose based on the work. A local service business can start with one market, a short list of buyer questions and a clear enquiry event. A larger team may need more markets, roles and reporting. Start with a trial that reflects the workload you actually expect.
