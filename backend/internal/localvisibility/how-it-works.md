# How it works

1. Open **Local Visibility** and type your business name (add the city). Uplumo
   searches Google Maps in your project's country and lists what it finds.
2. Pick your business and say what customers search to find it, such as
   "dentist". Saving is free.
3. **Profile vs top 3**: one click checks Google Maps for that search at your
   address and reads your profile and the three businesses above or around
   you. You get your rank, a list of fixes (categories, attributes, photos,
   posts, reviews, missing details) that the competitors' own profiles show,
   and a side-by-side table.
4. **Map rank grid**: pick 3 x 3 or 5 x 5 points, 0.5 to 5 km apart. Each
   circle is your rank at that point; click one to see the top 3 there.
5. **Reviews**: your last 100 reviews and 50 of each competitor's. You see
   your reply rate, the themes customers praise or complain about, and every
   review without a reply, with a reply draft you can copy.
6. **Competitor watch**: off until you turn it on. It snapshots the top 3 now
   and every week, and shows new reviews, rating changes, new categories and
   hours changes.
7. **AI answers**: write the question your customers ask an AI assistant and
   check it in Prompt Explorer or track it weekly in AI Visibility.

Results are kept; the same check within 24 hours shows the saved answer for
free. Every button shows the most it can charge before you click.
