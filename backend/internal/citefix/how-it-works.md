# Cite Fix: how it works for a user

1. Run an AI Visibility check (AI Visibility Tracker) and a free site scan
   (Site Audit). Cite Fix reads both.
2. The top says how many AI answers name your site in the latest check, and
   what changed: an AI that stopped naming you, an AI that never has, and
   where you are strongest.
3. "What to do next" lists only the moves your data points to, in order:
   scan, win back a lost question, get listed on pages that name your rivals,
   then your top site fix. Each shows its evidence and its cost.
4. "Each question, each AI" is a grid: lime = named now (with its spot),
   pink = named before but not now, dash = never. Click a cell to see who AI
   cited instead.
5. "Who AI picks instead" ranks rivals by how often and how high AI names
   them. "Pages that list your rivals, not you" are directories and lists AI
   quoted; ask their owners to add you.
6. "What their pages have" compares rival pages with your strongest scanned
   page: an answer in the first lines, FAQ, structured data, numbers, author,
   last update and length.
7. With a current scan you also get your score, topic clusters, the fix list
   and the AI agent that writes fixes for chosen pages (credits shown first).
   A page found at several addresses (`?from=home`) is one row. Byline,
   date, quotes and question headings are only expected on articles; service,
   product, contact and home pages are not marked missing for them. Topics
   appear only when the scan finds at least one.
8. History tables, mentions, Google AI Overview answers and SEO issues are
   under "All citation data".
