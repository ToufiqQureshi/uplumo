# How it works

1. Type your website (or any page) and press **Run free audit**. No account.
2. In about 10 seconds you see a score out of 100 and four category scores:
   On-page SEO, AI search (AEO), Technical, Content.
3. **Fix these first** lists the five most important problems with the fix.
4. Every check shows what we found, why it matters and how to fix it; failed
   checks open by default.
5. **Go further** cards suggest Uplumo tools for what the audit found (AI
   visibility when the AI score is low, the content editor when SEO or content
   is low, the full site audit and rank tracking always), with the plan and price.
6. The same page shows the same report for 24 hours.
